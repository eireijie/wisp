## What's new

- **Marketplace templates.** Ready-made agents (Daily, Work, Coding, Business, Research) and teams now live in Marketplace. Use one and it opens already filled in.
- **Agents know their apps.** When you connect an app, your agents are told about it and what it can do.
- **Apps across devices.** An agent can use an app on another of your devices while that device is online, for example a Windows agent using an app on your Mac.
- **Runs on.** Choose which of your devices a new agent runs on, and change it later in its profile.
- **All settings.** Every setting for an agent, including ones made during setup, is one tap from its profile.
- **Pins.** Only pinned agents sit in the pinned row. Your first agent is pinned once, and Unpin works everywhere.
- **Introduce yourself.** The last setup step now asks you to introduce yourself to your agent.
- **Away from home.** Setup now includes the step for reaching your agents away from home.
- **Show password.** Every password and key field has an eye to show what you typed.
- **Renaming.** Names you can edit now look like fields.
- **Composio.** Shows when no apps are picked yet, with a link to pick them.
