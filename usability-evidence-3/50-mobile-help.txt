  - region "Words you'll see":
    - heading "Words you'll see" [level=2]
    - term: Agent (helper)
    - definition: An AI helper you give work to, like a teammate. It has a name, remembers things, and works on a device.
    - term: Harness
    - definition: The AI program a helper runs on, like Claude Code or Codex. You pick it once when you make a helper.
    - term: Device
    - definition: A computer of yours with Warren installed. Helpers do their work there.
    - term: Computer
    - definition: A desktop a helper can see and use, either a virtual one on your device or your own screen.
    - term: Memory
    - definition: Notes Warren keeps about you and your work, so helpers don't need to be told twice. You can read and fix them.
    - term: Skill
    - definition: Something a helper has learned how to do, like a recipe it can follow again.
    - term: Routine
    - definition: A job a helper does on its own, on a schedule or when something happens.
    - term: Team
    - definition:
      - text: A group of helpers that work on something together.
      - link "Open Teams":
        - /url: "#/teams"
    - term: Connected app
    - definition: A service you link to Warren, like Gmail, so helpers can use it for you.
    - term: Token
    - definition: A unit AI services use to measure and charge for work. More text means more tokens.
