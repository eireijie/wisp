# Code of conduct

Warren is built in the open by people with different backgrounds, skills and opinions. To keep it a good place to work:

- Be kind and assume good intent. Critique ideas and code, never people.
- Welcome newcomers and questions, including basic ones.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination, or unwanted sexual attention, in any space connected to the project.
- Respect people's privacy: don't share someone's private information without their permission.

If someone's behavior makes you uncomfortable, tell the maintainer ([@eireijie](https://github.com/eireijie)) privately, or use GitHub's **Report content** on the comment. Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may remove comments, code or people that break these rules.
